## 2. Problem Validated dari Riset (dengan Sumber)

1. **Polished ads gagal, raw menang:** OPPO tutorial TikTok > hard sell [3]; casual/humor/daily-life = winning TikTok ID [4]; AI slop dihukum (Sora dihentikan Apr 2026 [2]) — tapi 90% UMKM Jatim masih posting katalog foto studio.
2. **Biaya iklan mahal di channel salah:** Meta CPA $38.33 vs TikTok $18.94 [3]; CPM TikTok $4.08 vs LinkedIn $5.74 CPC [3]; Meta spend +47% tapi ROAS turun 5.48→4.57x [13] — UMKM bakar uang di Meta broad karena tidak tahu alternatif.
3. **Creator mismatch:** Brand sewa macro 1M+ (ER 1-3%) padahal nano ER 8.1% TikTok [7] dan 67% orang ID beli karena creator genuine [6] — UMKM butuh *tetangga*, bukan selebriti.
4. **Social = search & toko:** TikTok search 2× lipat sejak 2024 [15]; 80-90% konversi Christie Basil dari TikTok [8]; social commerce 25% e-commerce ID [7] — tapi UMKM tidak punya konten yang *searchable*.
5. **79% UKM sudah pakai AI** [7] tapi hanya 12% optimasi GEO [12] — artinya mereka sudah *bayar* AI tapi tidak *ditemukan* AI. Peluang bundling: konten + GEO.

## 3. Solusi — Apa itu Studio Anomali?

**One-liner:** Pabrik 8 video/bulan/UMKM — dari riset outlier 20× → script hook 0:03 → shoot HP → edit motion → post → TikTok Shop ads → monitor ROAS — dengan karakter **Anomali lokal** (misal: "Bakso Anomali Banyuanyar", "Lele Hipdut") sebagai IP yang bisa di-lisensi.

**3 Paket:**
- **Starter $350/mo** — 4 video (1/minggu) + riset outlier + caption SEO TikTok
- **Growth $850/mo** — 8 video + 1 karakter Anomali custom + ads setup $200 included + weekly report (target CPA <$20)
- **Scale $1.800/mo** — 12 video + 2 karakter + LIVE 2×/bulan + affiliate management + GEO landing page (paling laku untuk kuliner/fashion Jatim)

**Tambahan:** Affiliate 10-20% dari TikTok Shop [4]; LIVE gifts; brand deal via TikTok One [11].

**Berbeda dari agency biasa:**
- Agency jual *jam kerja* — Anomali jual *lift vs median* (kontrak: minimal 1 video 3× median dalam 30 hari atau gratis 2 video)
- Agency pakai *feeling* — Anomali pakai **ScrapeCreators API** (outlier-post-finder median-baseline, comment-mining VOC, transcript hooks)
- Agency kasih laporan PDF — Anomali kasih **Ads Cockpit live** (12 platform audit, coverage 80/60%, 6-gate optimize)

## 6. Monetisasi & Unit Economics

**Harga Jatim (test 10 pilot):**
- Starter $350, Growth $850 (hero), Scale $1.800 — murahkan 30% vs agency Surabaya ($500/$1.200/$2.500) karena 60% research di-automasi AI (FMCG 3 orang ganti 8 [2]).

**Hitungan Growth $850:**
- COGS: Creator nano $120 + editor $150 + ads $200 (client) + tool $30 = $300 → **Gross $550 (65%)**
- + Affiliate 15% dari GMV: jika 1 video 50K views × 1.5% CTR Shop × 2% CVR = 15 orders × $8 avg × 15% = $18/video × 8 = $144/mo → **Upside**
- CAC via TikTok $18 [3] + LinkedIn tidak perlu (B2C lokal) → payback 1 bulan
- LTV 6 bulan $5.100 → **LTV:CAC 15×**

**Target 90 hari:**
- Pilot 5 UMKM (2 Banyuanyar, 2 Surabaya, 1 Malang) × $350 = $1.750 MRR
- Upsell 3 → Growth $850 = $4.300 MRR (churn 0 karena ada kontrak lift 3×)
- Affiliate $500 → **$4.800 MRR run-rate**
- Proof: 1 video 20× (misal: Lele Hipdut 400K views [6] style) → case study → 10 inbound

**Channel:**
- Offline: Pasar Banyuanyar, komunitas PKK, Karang Taruna — demo outlier di HP (trust > ads)
- Online: TikTok search "bakso Malang viral" — GEO content terstruktur → dikutip ChatGPT
- Ads: TikTok $4 CPM [3] untuk awareness + Meta retarget + Google "toko bakso terdekat"

# About the Author

**Setyo Wisodo** is an Indonesian technologist who works at the intersection of artificial intelligence, product management and growth strategy. His work focuses on building digital products — from generative-AI applications and intelligent automation to data-driven decision tools — that help people and organisations make better choices with better information.

*Surviving the Future* grew out of his conviction that the most important information of our century — what climate change means for ordinary families, and what they can do about it — should be clear, trustworthy and accessible to everyone. He lives in Indonesia.

Readers can follow his work at github.com/SetyoWisodo.

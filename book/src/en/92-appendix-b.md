# Appendix B: Emergency Contacts and Information Sources (Indonesia)

::: plain

Numbers and addresses were correct at the time of writing. Check with your local government for regional numbers, and save them in your phone and on paper.

## B.1 Emergency numbers

| Service | Number |
|---|---|
| National emergency call centre (all emergencies) | **112** |
| Police | 110 |
| Fire brigade | 113 |
| Search and rescue (Basarnas) | 115 |
| National Disaster Management Agency (BNPB) | 117 |
| Ambulance / medical emergency | 118 or 119 |
| Natural disaster command post | 129 |
| State electricity company (PLN) | 123 |

The 112 service operates under Ministry of Communication regulations and is run by local governments, which connect it to fire, health, police, BPBD and other services. It can be dialled free of charge from mobile phones.

| OJK | Registered financial institutions and lenders; consumer complaints | ojk.go.id; call centre 157 |

## B.3 International sources for further reading

- **IPCC** (ipcc.ch) — assessment reports on climate science, impacts, adaptation and mitigation; the Summaries for Policymakers are the most accessible entry point.
- **WMO** (wmo.int) — annual *State of the Global Climate* reports and early warning initiatives.
- **The *Lancet* Countdown** (lancetcountdown.org) — annual indicators on health and climate change.
- **Copernicus Climate Change Service** (climate.copernicus.eu) — monthly climate bulletins and data.
- **Indicators of Global Climate Change** (igcc.earth) — annual updates of key climate indicators and the remaining carbon budget.
- **World Health Organization** (who.int) — guidance on heat, health emergencies and psychological first aid.
- **AHA Centre** (ahacentre.org) — ASEAN coordination on disaster management.

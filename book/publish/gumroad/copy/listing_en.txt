SURVIVING THE FUTURE
Crisis, Climate, and Human Resilience
=====================================

The five years from 2020 to 2024 were the warmest ever measured. In 1970 a
cyclone killed up to half a million people in the Bengal delta. In 2020 a storm
of comparable strength hit the same coast: more than two million people were
moved to safety first, and the death toll was in the dozens.

The hazard did not change. The system did.

This book is about that gap. Across twenty-four chapters it examines heat,
flood, drought, fire, food, water, migration and conflict, and asks the same
question each time: what decides whether the same shock kills or spares a
community?


WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT
-------------------------

Every chapter ends by naming the observation that would prove it wrong.

That is not a rhetorical flourish. It is a commitment: each argument in this
book is stated precisely enough to be falsifiable, and the evidence that would
overturn it is written down where you can check it.

* 24 chapters, 430 pages
* 25 data tables and 11 figures, each traceable to a named source
* 145 sources, with notes on the provenance of every event figure
* 24 case studies: Bhola 1970, Amphan 2020, Chicago 1995, the Ahr valley 2021,
  Cape Town's water crisis, Chennai, and more
* Emergency checklists and a local risk profile you can fill in for your own street
* Glossary, analytical caveats, and a full index

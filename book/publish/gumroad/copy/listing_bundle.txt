WHY TWO EDITIONS AND NOT ONE TRANSLATION
-----------------------------------------

A translation substitutes words. These editions rebuild the argument for each
readership: the case studies that carry the most weight in Indonesia are the
ones developed at length in the Indonesian edition, and the Indonesian edition
carries its own citations, its own glossary, and its own references to local
policy.

Both editions carry the same falsifiable structure: every chapter ends by naming
the observation that would prove it wrong.

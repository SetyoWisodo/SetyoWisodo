SURVIVING THE FUTURE
Crisis, Climate, and Human Resilience

The five years from 2020 to 2024 were the warmest ever measured. In 1970 a cyclone killed up to half a million people in the Bengal delta. In 2020 a storm of comparable strength hit the same coast: more than two million people were moved to safety first, and the death toll was in the dozens.

The hazard did not change. The system did.

This book is about that gap. Across 24 chapters it examines heat, flood, drought, fire, food, water, migration and conflict, and asks one question each time: what decides whether the same shock kills or spares a community?

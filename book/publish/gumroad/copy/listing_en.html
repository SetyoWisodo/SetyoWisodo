SURVIVING THE FUTURE

Crisis, Climate, and Human Resilience

The five years from 2020 to 2024 were the warmest ever measured. In 1970 a cyclone killed up to half a million people in the Bengal delta. In 2020 a storm of comparable strength hit the same coast: more than two million people were moved to safety first, and the death toll was in the dozens.

The hazard did not change. The system did.

This book is about that gap. Across twenty-four chapters it examines heat, flood, drought, fire, food, water, migration and conflict, and asks the same question each time: what decides whether the same shock kills or spares a community?

WHAT MAKES THIS DIFFERENT

Every chapter ends by naming the observation that would prove it wrong.

That is not a rhetorical flourish. It is a commitment: each argument here is stated precisely enough to be falsified, and the evidence that would overturn it is written down where you can check it. You will not find that in a report written to be approved rather than tested.
